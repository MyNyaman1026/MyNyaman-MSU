Cyberbullying Support Guide
Cyberbullying can appear as repeated insults, threats, public embarrassment, exclusion, impersonation, or pressure to share private information. MyNyaman helps parents respond early by combining live alerts, usage patterns, child reports, and direct access to support.
🛡️
Pause before reactingSave evidence, reassure your child, and avoid replying to harmful messages in anger.
💬
Open the conversationAsk what happened, who was involved, and whether the child feels unsafe or pressured.
🚨
Escalate serious harmUse SOS or consultant chat when there are threats, blackmail, stalking, or repeated harassment.
